# Research & Data

Use this Skill only when the task depends on research-data semantics rather than generic repository work.

## Domain rules

- Preserve source identity, units, periods, geography, version/edition, null semantics, and provenance.
- Keep raw/canonical inputs separate from generated views and derived results.
- Deduplicate by real identity, not display-string similarity alone.
- Distinguish source claims, direct observations, derived results, inference, and unknown.
- When reproducing or evaluating a result, report the actual sample, inclusion/exclusion rules, period, uncertainty, and failure cases that affect interpretation.
- Compare against the relevant baseline or published reference using directly comparable definitions.
- Preserve enough evidence to reproduce why each canonical record or result exists.
- Do not claim empirical, field, production, adoption, or monetization outcomes that were not directly observed.

## Done

Done means the research result or reusable data artifact is traceable to its inputs and can be checked against the question it was meant to answer.
