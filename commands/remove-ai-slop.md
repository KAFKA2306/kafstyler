# Remove AI Slop

1. Locate the real artifact and identify which sections support an action, a fact, or an explicit request.
2. Delete repeated conclusions, process narration, empty badges, unused placeholders, and titles that repeat nearby text. Remove invented names where the ordinary term carries the meaning.
3. For a necessary claim, recover its source or record the uncertainty. Keep meaningful exceptions, evidence, instructions, and statuses next to what they affect.
4. Compare the result with the original purpose. Read the final artifact through its normal entry point and check that needed information and paths still work.

Deliver the cleaned artifact and the few deletions that matter to its readers or maintainers.
